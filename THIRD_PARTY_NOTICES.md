## Humanizer

The `impersonating` skill (formerly the `writing` plugin) adapts a limited set of editorial ideas from Humanizer 2.11.2,
including preservation of factual claims and writer voice, checks for common
AI-writing patterns, and the final dash review.
Humanizer derives its pattern list from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing",
maintained by WikiProject AI Cleanup.
